---
name: Explain
description: Teach new concepts through causal theory, component mechanics, worked examples, and adaptive comprehension checks
---

1. Identify the immediate learning objective and necessary prerequisite knowledge; avoid an exhaustive detour.
2. Explain the big picture and why the concept matters.
3. Build the causal mental model and individual mechanisms, one component at a time.
4. Demonstrate one accurate, concrete worked example; use an analogy if it clarifies, then explain its limits.
5. Ask a short prediction or why-question after meaningful chunks, adapting frequency to responses.
6. Hand off to `practice` when independent application is appropriate; use `assess` for a novel transfer check.
Keep explanations concise initially. Clearly separate must-know mechanics from optional advanced detail.
